# V3.2 Local rotor to global enclosure placement contract

## Status and scope

This document defines the coordinate boundary between the inherited AS5600 rotor mechanism and a future enclosure layout. It is a source-level engineering contract, not a dimensioned CAD release. It intentionally does not assign guessed enclosure coordinates or generate geometry.

Branch: `cla`
Scope: `mechanical_design/` only.

## 1. Authoritative functional arrangement

The following constraints are fixed for future layout work:

- The AS5600-linked one-piece rotor operating disc is horizontal in the XY plane and rotates about Z.
- It is at the upper-right exterior edge of the enclosure. A portion of its circumference extends beyond the enclosure boundary for finger access.
- The AS5600 PCB is horizontal; its sensing package faces downward (-Z) toward the magnet.
- AS5600 package, magnet and rotor shaft share one axis.
- The one-piece rotor retains its external operating disc, through-wall journal, internal retaining flange, magnet carrier and Leica linkage rod.
- The E8H7 menu encoder is a separate mechanism and must never be used as the identity or placement reference for this rotor.
- The LCD stays on the left with USB-C facing the left exterior wall. TSL2591 stays front-facing (+Y), with its complete module envelope below the AS5600/rotor axis level as required.
- The enclosure may grow. Do not move the rotor inward, reduce its useful exposed circumference, shrink the linkage sweep or change the mechanism relationship to force a compact footprint.

For this coordinate contract, +X means the device's right, +Y means the device's front, and +Z means upward toward the top cover. Confirm this handedness against the physical device before entering measured global coordinates.

## 2. Local rotor coordinate frame

The rotor mechanism must be authored in a local frame whose origin is the common rotor / AS5600 / magnet axis:

- Local origin: rotor axis center.
- Local XY plane: plane of the external operating disc.
- Local +Z: toward the AS5600 PCB and magnet carrier.
- Local -Z: toward the external operating disc and camera linkage side.
- The local mechanism may remain centered at (0, 0) in its own frame. This is not the enclosure origin.

The V0.9/V1.0/V1.7 macros are historical local mechanism references. Their centered test coupons do not provide a valid global placement. In particular, V1.7 also embeds unrelated provisional global placements (including the display at positive X); those placements are explicitly not to be inherited.

## 3. Global placement transform

For the current intended architecture, the rotor local frame is translated in XY into the upper-right enclosure-edge region. Do not rotate the local rotor frame or change its Z-axis direction as part of this placement.

For a point in the local rotor frame:

`P_global = (x_local + X_rotor, y_local + Y_rotor, z_local + Z_rotor)`

Where:

- `X_rotor`: measured/designed global X coordinate of the common rotor axis.
- `Y_rotor`: measured/designed global Y coordinate of the common rotor axis.
- `Z_rotor`: global Z coordinate of the local rotor origin, derived from the measured sensor/magnet/rotor stack.
- `X_rotor`, `Y_rotor`, and `Z_rotor` are required inputs. They have no defaults and must not be inferred from V0.9/V1.0 coupon centers or V2.0/V2.9 previews.

A future FreeCAD macro must fail closed if any required global placement input is absent. It may generate a local rotor-only study without global placement, but it must label that output as a local mechanism coupon and must not call it an integrated enclosure layout.

## 4. Edge overhang and access parameters

The exposed-disc placement must be controlled by explicit enclosure-edge and rotor parameters, not a hard-coded center guess.

Required inputs:

| Parameter | Definition | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `ENCLOSURE_X_RIGHT` | Global X coordinate of the finished right exterior wall | Pending |
| `ENCLOSURE_Y_FRONT` | Global Y coordinate of the finished front exterior wall | Pending |
| `ROTOR_X_GLOBAL` | Global X coordinate of common rotor axis | Pending |
| `ROTOR_Y_GLOBAL` | Global Y coordinate of common rotor axis | Pending |
| `ROTOR_DISC_DIAMETER` | Actual external operating-disc diameter | Pending physical confirmation |
| `ROTOR_DISC_THICKNESS` | Actual external operating-disc thickness | Pending physical confirmation |
| `EXPOSED_OVERHANG_RIGHT` | Disc extent beyond right wall: `ROTOR_X_GLOBAL + R - ENCLOSURE_X_RIGHT` | Derived after inputs |
| `EXPOSED_OVERHANG_FRONT` | Disc extent beyond front wall: `ROTOR_Y_GLOBAL + R - ENCLOSURE_Y_FRONT` | Derived after inputs |
| `FINGER_CLEARANCE` | Required unobstructed access volume around exposed disc | Pending ergonomic definition |
| `WALL_CUTOUT_CLEARANCE` | Running clearance between rotating disc/journal and stationary enclosure | Pending process/material selection |

Here `R = ROTOR_DISC_DIAMETER / 2`. Positive overhang means the disc crosses the corresponding exterior-wall plane. The exact intended exposed sides and minimum usable exposed arc must be confirmed from the physical camera orientation and operating gesture; do not assume that positive overhang alone proves adequate finger access.

The wall opening, adjacent corner radius, top-cover edge, base edge and finger-access envelope must be evaluated together. The exposed disc must not be accidentally boxed in by the top cover or an adjacent accessory.

## 5. Local mechanism inheritance and cautions

The historical local rotor architecture is useful as a starting point:

- One-piece rotor: external disc + journal + retaining flange + magnet carrier + linkage rod.
- Separate stationary items: sleeve bushing, lower/upper thrust washers and fixed retainer.
- V1.7 keyed-washer concept: 1.0 mm nominal key, 1.15 mm notch width, 0.15 mm shallow upper pocket, and a 0.20 mm nominal axial-endplay target.

These are reference values only. The V1.7 macro's AS5600 PCB, package, magnet, rotor, base and retainer dimensions are still provisional. Its 0.20 mm endplay is a target, not a validated tolerance result. The V1.7 macro also contains a 15 mm stack witness whose interpretation is explicitly unconfirmed.

Before reusing the local geometry, resolve:

1. Actual external disc diameter/thickness and linkage-rod offset/length/material.
2. Actual journal and bushing dimensions, retention and installed fit.
3. Actual shell-wall thickness and bore/cutout geometry.
4. Actual magnet size, magnetization, retention and package-face-to-magnet-surface gap.
5. Actual washer thicknesses, retainer seat height and assembled axial endplay.
6. Linkage rod's full 360-degree sweep relative to the Leica body and enclosure.
7. Tool access, retainer screw-boss strength and service/removal sequence.

Do not infer the sensor gap from the PCB plane or magnet-carrier top. Measure from the actual AS5600 package sensing face to the magnet surface.

## 6. Global layout dependencies

A future integrated layout must use independently measured component envelopes and preserve these relationships:

- LCD/board envelope on the left; USB-C opening and cable insertion envelope at the left wall.
- TSL2591 complete PCB/component envelope facing +Y, below the rotor-axis Z level, with a continuous optical path.
- AS5600 board horizontal and package-down, with magnet and rotor axis coaxial.
- E8H7 horizontal shaft/control support independent of the AS5600 rotor and with its own load path.
- Battery volume remains uncommitted until a real battery and replacement strategy are selected.
- Bottom shell carries mechanical loads. The cover is protective and must not clamp the LCD PCB or carry encoder/rotor operating loads.
- PCB modules are independently supported and are not structural members.

The global XY layout cannot be solved from the historical 96 x 61 mm V2.0 chassis. That chassis was built around the wrong centered rotor placement and wrong display-side assumptions. Enlarge or reshape the enclosure to satisfy the functional arrangement.

## 7. Required validation before integrated CAD

1. Confirm the global coordinate handedness on the actual device.
2. Measure the enclosure/interface reference edges and the Leica camera contact/sweep geometry.
3. Measure the actual rotor disc and linkage hardware.
4. Choose and measure the magnet, AS5600 package datum, bushing and thrust parts.
5. Enter explicit `X_rotor`, `Y_rotor`, `Z_rotor`, wall-edge and access inputs.
6. Calculate right/front overhang and inspect the exposed arc and finger-clearance volume.
7. Check 360-degree rotor and linkage sweeps against the camera, enclosure, TSL2591 module and all fasteners.
8. Check local Z stack and axial endplay from measured mating faces.
9. Only then generate the integrated FreeCAD model and separately validate solid validity, collisions, STEP export and physical prototypes.

## 8. Validation boundary

This document is based on source inspection of the committed V0.9, V1.0 and V1.7 macros and the V2.8/V3.1 mechanical records. No FreeCAD macro was executed for this revision. No FCStd/STEP was generated, no solid validity or interference result is claimed, and no new physical measurement is claimed.
