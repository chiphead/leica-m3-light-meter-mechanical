## Corrected mechanism interpretation

The selected candidate is **F-SWITCH E8H8-7.5J30-9B15-F200**, an external-mount horizontal SMT encoder. Its shaft axis is parallel to the PCB plane. The existing approximately Ø20 mm thin printed wheel is mounted on the Ø3 mm shaft; the wheel rotates for menu navigation and is pushed axially to actuate the encoder's own independent-line CLICK switch.

This replaces the incorrect V1.1 assumption of a vertical encoder shaft with a separate concentric center-button plunger. No hollow shaft or separate center plunger is required by this encoder concept.

Manufacturer drawing/specification: [F-SWITCH E8H8-7.5J30-9B15-F200 product approval specification](https://dicomon-shop.oss-cn-shenzhen.aliyuncs.com/media/store/1001/file/20231204/1701680562979998.pdf). Drawing number FSE8-WI-DR-065.

| Output | 9 pulses / 360° |
| Detents | 18 / 360°, 20° ±3° each |

The drawing identifies a separate electrical push-switch circuit (D-E). The click contact is electrically independent from the A/B quadrature contacts. It is still actuated mechanically through the encoder shaft/wheel, not by a separate center plunger.

## Model assumptions (not drawing-confirmed)

- PCB outline 10.3 x 10.0 mm and nominal 1.0 mm thickness; actual footprint drawing must be followed.
- Encoder body height 4.4 mm and shaft projection 2.5 mm are layout estimates interpreted from the drawing; verify from the purchased component and its PCB stack.
- Existing wheel Ø20 x 1.5 mm is retained as an approximate envelope.
- Hub Ø5 x 2 mm with Ø3.1 mm bore is a coupling concept only. A clamp, split hub, set screw or adhesive retention method has not been selected.
- Support plate dimensions and housing reaction stop are conceptual. The support must react the 200 gf push load into the enclosure, while avoiding force on solder terminals and the PCB laminate.

## Mechanical design requirements

1. Keep the shaft axis parallel to the encoder PCB plane; do not rotate this component into the previous vertical-shaft arrangement.
2. Wheel rotation and axial press use the same wheel and shaft. The wheel needs a guided axial stroke of approximately 0.2 mm without binding.
3. Provide a hard stop so the printed wheel/hub cannot overtravel the encoder's specified push travel. The stop must react into the enclosure bracket, not into the solder terminals.
4. The wheel hub must transmit torque without imposing excessive radial load or bending moment on the Ø3 shaft.
5. Preserve access to all SMT terminals and avoid using solder joints as the sole mechanical retention.
6. Provide clearance around the wheel for finger access and prevent the enclosure from rubbing the wheel during its 0.2 mm axial movement.
7. Confirm the shaft's actual axial movement, restoring force and switch operation on a physical sample before finalizing the bracket.
