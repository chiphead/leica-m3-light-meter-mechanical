## What changed from V0.9

V0.9 showed only an inner-side thrust washer near the shaft, which did not support the annular contact region where the retaining ring overlaps the rotor flange. V1.0 separates the two axial interfaces:
- **Lower thrust washer:** between the stationary shell underside and the rotating external operating disc.
- **Upper thrust washer:** between the rotating internal flange perimeter and the stationary screw-fixed retaining ring.

The sleeve bushing remains the radial guide. It should not be used to set axial preload. The retaining ring is a keeper, not a clamp: its mounting height and shim stack must leave measurable axial endplay.

## Provisional stack

Datum: shell underside Z=0; shell top Z=2 mm. The external disc is below the shell. The shaft journal passes through the lower washer, bushing and shell bore; the inner flange and magnet carrier sit above the shell.

Key trial values: shell bore Ø8.4 mm; bushing OD/ID 8.2/6.2 mm; journal Ø6 mm; lower washer 24/8.4 x 0.3 mm; flange Ø24 x 2 mm; upper washer 22/20.5 x 0.3 mm; retainer 28/20.5 x 1.2 mm; retainer screw PCD 25.5 mm. These are illustrative only.

## Critical engineering caveat

The macro models nominal touching faces. It does not yet implement a tolerance stack or a controlled axial endplay. In the real design, establish endplay using measured shell thickness, rotor dimensions, washer thickness, retainer seat height and any shims. Do not tighten the retainer against the rotating stack. Verify low breakaway torque, no perceptible rocking, no axial rattle beyond the chosen limit, and no binding through 360 degrees.

Both thrust washers need a material with suitable wear and friction properties; printed plastic-on-plastic contact is only a prototype experiment. A thin PTFE, POM or suitable engineered-polymer washer may be evaluated, but material selection must consider creep, wear, temperature and availability.

## Unresolved interfaces

- Actual Ø1 mm linkage rod material and attachment strength.
- Actual magnet dimensions, polarity and pocket retention.
- AS5600 package-face to magnet-face gap; do not infer it from the carrier top.
- Retainer screw bosses and their load path into the bottom shell.
- Camera clearance and swept envelope of the external rod.
- Manufacturing tolerances and print orientation.
