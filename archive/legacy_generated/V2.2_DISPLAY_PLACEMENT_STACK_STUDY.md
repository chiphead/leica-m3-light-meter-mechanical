# V2.2 display placement and vertical-stack reconciliation

## Purpose

V2.2 converts the V2.1 findings into a coordinate-level placement study. It does not change V2.0 geometry or pretend that the display can simply be shifted to the left wall. The goal is to establish a mathematically consistent candidate and expose the remaining physical conflicts before editing the assembly macro.

## Fixed references

- Chassis floor XY bounds: X=-38..58, Y=-29..32 mm.
- Chassis floor Z bounds from V2.0's current rotor stack: Z=-10.3..-8.3 mm.
- Display board nominal envelope: 37 × 21 × 8 mm.
- Display glass outer face datum: Z=0.
- V0.6 board-local convention: glass thickness 0.6 mm, board body Z=-8.6..-0.6 mm.
- USB-C on board's left X edge; provisional shell projection 3.5 mm toward -X.
- USB shell Y span in board-local coordinates: 6.0..15.0 mm; center Y=10.5 mm.
- USB shell Z span: -7.55..-4.35 mm.
- V2.0 front TSL2591 PCB envelope: X=-30..-14, Y=23..28 mm.
- V2.0 AS5600 PCB envelope: X/Y=-11.5..11.5 mm, Z=0..1.6 mm.

## Candidate A — move display to the left wall without rotation

To align the board's left connector edge to the inside face of the left chassis wall, translate the board-local origin to approximately X=-36 mm (wall inner face is X=-36 mm). Preserve the existing board Y center at -4 mm, so the board-local Y origin is -14.5 mm.

Derived display board envelope:
- X=-36..1 mm
- Y=-14.5..6.5 mm
- Z=-8.6..-0.6 mm

Derived USB shell envelope:
- X=-39.5..-36 mm
- Y=-8.5..0.5 mm
- Z=-7.55..-4.35 mm

This aligns the shell projection to the left wall region in a simple rectangular model. The nominal opening would be 9.6 mm along Y × 3.8 mm along Z, centered at Y=-4 mm and Z=-5.95 mm. The wall is 2 mm thick; connector insertion axis and actual shell-to-board setback still require physical measurement.

### Candidate A XY overlap warning

The translated display PCB overlaps the AS5600 PCB in XY:
- Display X overlap: -11.5..1 mm (13 mm).
- Display Y overlap: -11.5..6.5 mm (18 mm).
- The common XY region is therefore 13 × 18 mm.

Because the current V0.6-derived display board body is below Z=0 while the V2.0 AS5600 PCB begins at Z=0, the idealized board solids do not overlap vertically (nominal 0.6 mm separation). However, this does **not** prove a valid assembly: display glass/bezel, solder joints, connectors, board components, support ribs and the real AS5600 underside package are not included in this simplified check. The display also crosses the central rotor's XY footprint, so its underside must be checked against the rotor, magnet and retainer stack over the full swept region.

Candidate A is a placement hypothesis, not an approved layout.

## Vertical-stack reconciliation — current hard conflict

Using V2.0's present equations and provisional dimensions:

- AS5600 package bottom: Z=-1.5 mm.
- Magnet top: Z=-2.5 mm.
- Rotor carrier bottom: Z=-6.5 mm.
- Rotor flange bottom: Z=-8.4 mm.
- Rotor journal bottom: Z=-10.7 mm.
- External rotor disc bottom: Z=-12.6 mm.
- Chassis floor bottom/top: Z=-10.3 / -8.3 mm.

Compare the V0.6 display board body Z=-8.6..-0.6 mm:
- It intersects the chassis floor Z=-10.3..-8.3 by 0.3 mm in any shared XY region.
- The current V2.0 display support pads rise from the floor top Z=-8.3 to Z=0 and were built for a board at Z=0..8, not for the corrected board body.
- Therefore, the V2.0 display support pads cannot be retained as-is.
- Moving only the display board to the V0.6 Z position does not solve the floor intersection.

This is a genuine stack-up conflict in the current provisional model, not a cosmetic coordinate issue.

## What must change before a V2.3 geometry macro

1. Re-establish the global Z stack from the physical display glass face (Z=0), then place the chassis floor, rotor, AS5600 board/package, magnet and encoder around that datum. Do not preserve the current V2.0 floor Z merely because it was calculated from an earlier rotor coupon.
2. Keep the LCD glass near the top-cover exterior (initial recess target 0.4 mm); model the glass/bezel separately from the PCB.
3. Replace the four V2.0 tall LCD pads with local supports that contact safe PCB underside zones and avoid components, solder joints and USB-C.
4. Verify the display board's actual component-side orientation and underside component heights. The nominal 8 mm board envelope is not enough to define safe support contacts.
5. Confirm the actual connector shell position and plug dimensions. The 9.6 × 3.8 mm cutout remains provisional.
6. Check Candidate A's 13 × 18 mm XY overlap with AS5600 and its overlap with the rotor's full swept envelope using actual component solids or measured keep-outs.
7. If Candidate A cannot pass these checks, consider rotating the display board 180° in-plane or moving it to another wall; do not rotate it without also checking viewing orientation, connector direction and TSL2591 optical placement.

## V2.2 status

- Candidate A coordinates and simple envelope intersections are analytically derived from V0.6/V2.0 source parameters.
- The Z-stack values above are arithmetic evaluations of V2.0 equations, not FreeCAD runtime measurements.
- No CAD geometry has been changed or generated.
- No FreeCAD execution, shape validity, interference, actual PCB fit, plug access, optical performance or physical assembly is claimed.
- All dimensions other than the user's 7.55 mm display-to-USB-shell measurement remain provisional where noted.

**Decision:** V2.0 is not a reliable starting point for detailed display retention. V2.3 should rebuild the display and rotor vertical stack together, not patch the old display pads.
