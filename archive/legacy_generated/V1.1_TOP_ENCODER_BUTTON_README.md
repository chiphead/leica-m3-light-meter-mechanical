# V1.1 top encoder and independent center-button mechanism study

Run `build_v11_top_encoder_independent_button.FCMacro` in FreeCAD.

Outputs:
- `V1.1_Top_Encoder_Independent_Button.FCStd`
- `V1.1_Top_Encoder_Independent_Button.step`

## Purpose

This is an isolated mechanism study for the top control stack. It preserves the existing approximately Ø20 mm dial envelope and does not redesign its appearance. The encoder body is represented by an 8 x 8 x 8 mm placeholder only.

The AS5600 PCB is shown below the encoder for spatial context. It is a reference envelope, **not** a structural support. The encoder and button bracket must transfer user loads into reinforced enclosure bosses or a dedicated carrier.

## Independent force paths

- Dial rotation: dial -> replaceable hub/coupler -> encoder shaft.
- Button: stationary cap -> non-rotating plunger -> separate return spring -> mechanical travel stop -> support bracket -> enclosure bosses.
- The button cap/plunger must not rotate with the dial.
- The encoder PCB, AS5600 PCB and electrical switch must not be the primary reaction structure for button force.

## Critical unresolved coaxial interface

The model intentionally draws a **solid Ø4 mm shaft placeholder** and a separate concentric button guide/plunger candidate. They occupy the same central passage and therefore conflict. This is deliberate: a concentric center button is not mechanically established until the actual encoder is identified.

Possible solutions to evaluate after obtaining the part number:
1. Encoder with a genuinely hollow shaft and sufficient internal diameter/length for the plunger and its running clearance.
2. A separate concentric sleeve/hollow hub architecture, but only if the encoder shaft geometry and coupling allow it.
3. A non-concentric button location if the industrial design permits it.

Do not assume that a generic encoder shaft is hollow, and do not drill or modify an encoder shaft without confirming its construction and strength.

## Provisional dimensions

- Encoder body: 8 x 8 x 8 mm; unverified placeholder.
- Encoder shaft: Ø4 x 6 mm; unverified solid placeholder.
- Existing dial envelope: Ø20 x 1.5 mm; approximate reference only.
- Button travel: 0.6 mm; concept value, must match the selected switch operating travel and safe overtravel.
- Button plunger: Ø1.6 mm; guide ID Ø1.8 mm; illustrative clearance only.
- Support plate: 18 x 18 x 1.6 mm with four Ø2 mm fastener holes on Ø14 mm PCD; bracket concept only.

## Required measurements before detailed design

Obtain the encoder part number and measure body size, mounting method, shaft diameter/type/length, shaft center height, bushing/bearing projection and any anti-rotation features. Also identify the exact tactile switch and its operating force, pre-travel, total travel and allowable overtravel. Measure the available stack height above the AS5600 PCB and confirm the support bracket can anchor to the enclosure without loading either PCB.
