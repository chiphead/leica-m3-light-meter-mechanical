## Scope

V1.4 advances the V1.3 spatial reference by integrating the V1.0 dual-thrust rotor arrangement into the same assembly. The selected top encoder remains **F-SWITCH E8H7-6.5J30-9B15-F200**. Per user instruction, encoder pins, solder pads and exact PCB footprint are not modeled; the encoder is a coarse envelope only.

## Coordinate system and rotor stack

- XY is horizontal; +Z points toward the enclosure top.
- The AS5600 PCB is horizontal; its sensor package faces -Z.
- Magnet and rotor carrier share the AS5600 sensing axis.
- The bottom rotor is one conceptual fused rotating solid: magnet carrier, flange, journal, external disc and vertical Ø1 mm linkage rod.
- The stationary shell coupon surrounds the journal. A replaceable sleeve bushing is fitted in its central bore and provides radial guidance only.
- The lower thrust washer sits between shell underside and external operating disc.
- The upper thrust washer sits between the rotating flange rim and the stationary retaining ring.
- The retaining ring has four screw holes on a 25.5 mm PCD and is supported by four bosses integrated into the stationary shell coupon.
- The retaining ring is a keeper, not a clamp. The current macro models nominal touching faces and does **not** establish a controlled axial endplay.
- E8H7 remains a separate menu encoder. Its horizontal shaft and vertical Ø20 mm wheel do not share the AS5600/rotor axis.

## Important stack observations

The macro derives the stationary base Z location from the existing V1.3 rotor coordinates so the lower washer nominally lies between the external disc and shell underside. The journal crosses the washer and shell/bushing region. The flange sits above the shell and the upper washer/retainer captures its outer rim while the retainer bore clears the Ø20 mm magnet carrier.

This is a **nominal geometry arrangement**, not a tolerance-controlled mechanism. In particular:

1. The bushing OD/ID clearances are nominal geometric values; actual printed or machined dimensions will vary.
2. The two thrust washers are shown touching their mating faces. There is no shim selection or controlled endplay yet.
3. The retaining ring screws and bosses are only coarse envelopes. Screw head access, thread engagement, inserts and wall thickness need a later design pass.
4. The magnet pocket/retention is not designed. Magnet polarity, material and actual size must be measured.
5. The Ø1 mm linkage rod is fused into the conceptual rotor for geometry; actual attachment strength and non-ferromagnetic material still require validation.
6. The AS5600 PCB is not a structural support. The E8H7 click load must be reacted by a separate enclosure bracket.
7. The TSL2591 optical path must remain clear. This reference placement does not validate field of view or stray-light performance.
8. Battery, final enclosure length/width, USB-C opening and screen-glass-to-cover relationship are not solved by this model.

## 15 mm target

The 15 mm witness is retained from V1.3 and runs from the AS5600 PCB underside datum (Z=0) to Z=15 mm. The user's intended measurement start/end faces are still unconfirmed. It is not an assertion that the whole mechanical stack or enclosure fits within 15 mm.

## Validation status

The macro has not been executed in FreeCAD in this workflow. FCStd/STEP export, Boolean validity, clearances, screw access, rotor freedom, and physical fit remain unverified. This is an editable reference model, not a manufacturing release.
