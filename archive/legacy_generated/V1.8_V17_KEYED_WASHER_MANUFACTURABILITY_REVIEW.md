# V1.8 V1.7 keyed-washer manufacturability review

## Scope

This is a static review of the V1.7 FreeCAD macro and its nominal dimensions. It does not modify the V1.7 geometry and is not a substitute for executing the macro, checking the resulting B-rep solids, or testing a printed assembly.

Reviewed source:
- `build_v17_e8h7_keyed_washer_assembly.FCMacro`
- V1.7 macro blob at review time: `ca7dae2dab15e13693b34aec543fcbd204e7c9fe`

The nominal upper thrust clearance is 0.20 mm. The lower washer is placed directly between the external-disc upper face and the shell underside. These results are arithmetic reconstructions from the macro, not measured CAD clearances.

## Findings

### 1. Lower washer and collar

- Collar ID Ø24.2 around washer OD Ø24.0 gives 0.20 mm diametral clearance (0.10 mm nominal radial clearance).
- The lower key occupies the same axial band as the collar and washer. Its 1.0 mm width is paired with a 1.15 mm notch width, giving 0.15 mm nominal total side clearance.
- The washer can conceptually be inserted axially from the underside into the collar before the rotor is installed. The rotor external disc then obstructs removal while assembled.
- The collar/key arrangement is a locating feature, not a positive axial latch. The washer is not independently retained when the rotor is removed.
- The key/notch outline is a rectangular approximation. Corner radii, print compensation, washer edge chamfers and real material tolerances are not represented.

### 2. Upper washer and rotor key

- The washer OD Ø22.0 in a nominal Ø22.2 locating recess gives 0.20 mm diametral clearance.
- The key is added after the recess cut and overlaps the flange region in the macro's intended geometry. The notch width is 1.15 mm against a 1.0 mm key.
- The key top is nominally 0.01 mm below the upper washer top. This avoids a nominal protrusion above the washer thrust face, but 0.01 mm is too small to treat as a robust manufacturing allowance without a tolerance study.
- The upper washer is only axially captured in the assembled stack. It is removable after the retainer is removed; it is not permanently locked.

### 3. Retainer and fasteners

- Retainer OD Ø32, ID Ø20.5 and thickness 1.2 mm are modeled.
- Four Ø2.2 mm holes on PCD25.5 are present in both the retainer and support-boss pattern.
- The boss OD is Ø4.5 mm. At the nominal hole radius, the boss has about 1.15 mm radial material outside a Ø2.2 mm hole; the retainer has about 1.05 mm radial material from the hole edge to its Ø32 outer boundary.
- Screw heads, head seats/counterbores, threaded inserts, thread engagement, screw length, driver access and tightening access are not modeled. The current macro therefore cannot establish that the retainer can be fastened or serviced with a real tool.
- The boss-to-retainer contact is represented by nominal touching surfaces. No explicit hard-stop shoulder or tolerance-controlled seating interface is modeled beyond the boss height.

### 4. Rotor and radial guide

- Retainer ID Ø20.5 around the rotor carrier OD Ø20 provides 0.50 mm diametral clearance (0.25 mm radial clearance).
- Bushing ID Ø6.2 around the Ø6.0 journal provides 0.20 mm diametral clearance.
- The bushing is a separate reference solid. Its retention against axial migration is not defined.
- Full 360-degree clearance of the linkage rod against the enclosure, collar, bosses, fasteners and other components cannot be checked from this local coupon alone.

### 5. Independent E8H7 mechanism

- The E8H7 wheel is represented on a separate horizontal axis. The macro does not intentionally couple it to the AS5600 rotor.
- The encoder support is only a coarse envelope. Its CLICK reaction load path into a dedicated chassis support is not structurally resolved in this local model.
- Wheel-to-shell clearance, finger access, shaft retention and button overtravel require a complete enclosure and actual E8H7 drawing/measurement.

## Assembly sequence suggested by the concept

1. Install the lower washer from the underside, aligning its notch with the collar key.
2. Install the rotor through the central guide, keeping the lower washer seated.
3. Fit the upper washer into the rotor flange recess, aligning its notch with the rotor key.
4. Position the retainer on its supports and verify the intended axial clearance with measured parts/shims.
5. Fasten the retainer only after screw-head seating and tool access have been designed.

This is a proposed sequence, not a verified assembly procedure. The exact direction and access for inserting the rotor must be checked against the final enclosure.

## Required V1.9 work

1. Add actual screw-head geometry, head seats/counterbores, insert or thread strategy, screw lengths and driver envelopes.
2. Define a positive, inspectable retainer hard-stop datum and ensure tightening cannot collapse the endplay.
3. Define bushing axial retention and a serviceable replacement method.
4. Add enclosure walls and a swept 360-degree envelope for the lower linkage rod and external operating disc.
5. Run the macro in the target FreeCAD version; check each shape for null/valid state, solid count and volume, then inspect intersections and export results.
6. Replace provisional module envelopes with measurements from the actual hardware before freezing the enclosure.

## Validation status

- V1.7 source was fetched and reviewed.
- Nominal Z positions and simple radial/diametral clearances were calculated from the source.
- No FreeCAD execution was performed as part of this review.
- No FCStd or STEP output was generated or inspected.
- Boolean validity, actual intersections, printability, tool access, assembly feasibility and physical fit remain unverified.
