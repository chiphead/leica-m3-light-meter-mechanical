# V2.0 full internal layout and open chassis reference

## Purpose

V2.0 moves from the local V1.9 rotor coupon to a single-coordinate-system internal layout study. It adds a provisional open chassis floor and perimeter rails, a front optical aperture, local LCD support pads, and an independent E8H7 reaction-support concept.

This is not a closed enclosure, a printable shell, or a released mechanical design. The footprint is a layout envelope inferred from the current component placements and still needs physical validation.

## Coordinate system and provisional chassis envelope

- XY is the horizontal layout plane; +Z points toward the top of the enclosure.
- The rotor / AS5600 axis remains at X=0, Y=0.
- The chassis floor is 96 × 61 × 2 mm, centered at (X=10, Y=1.5) mm.
- Nominal floor bounds: X=-38..58 mm; Y=-29..32 mm.
- The front perimeter rail is placed at Y=28..30 mm, with a 4 × 4 mm aperture centered at the TSL2591 optical package (X=-22 mm, Z=8 mm).
- Side and rear rails are 2 mm thick and 20 mm high in this layout study. The front wall is also 20 mm high, interrupted only by the optical aperture.
- The lid, corner radii, draft, wall thickness optimization, seams, gasket and fastener interfaces are not modeled.

The 96 × 61 mm floor is a provisional consequence of the current layout, not a user-approved final external size. It may change after the real module outlines, connectors and service clearances are measured.

## New support concepts

### E8H7 independent reaction structure

- A nominal 18 × 18 × 1.6 mm support plate is placed below the E8H7 reference board.
- Four 2 × 2 mm posts descend from the support plate to the chassis floor.
- Posts are positioned at X/Y = ±14 mm, outside the nominal 23 × 23 mm AS5600 PCB outline.
- This creates a conceptual force path from the encoder support to the chassis without using the AS5600 PCB as a structural member.

The post geometry has not been checked for strength, buckling, print orientation, local stress, assembly access or collision with the final PCB and wiring. The support plate is a concept, not a validated E8H7 footprint.

### LCD support

Four local support pads are placed beneath the approximate display-board corners. Their height is derived to reach the current display board bottom datum Z=0 from the chassis floor top. The pads do not imply that the board should be clamped by the upper cover. Screw locations, board keep-outs, USB-C connector clearance and insertion space still need actual measurements.

### TSL2591 optical front

The front rail contains a 4 × 4 mm opening aligned to the current TSL2591 package center. This only demonstrates a geometric passage through the rail. It does not establish field of view, spectral neutrality, stray-light suppression, window material suitability or the final optical channel geometry. Continue the V0.8 coupon testing before fixing this aperture.

## Important issues deliberately left open

1. **USB-C access:** the display board's USB-C connector is on its left side. The current chassis rails do not yet include a connector-specific side-wall opening or plug insertion envelope. Do not treat V2.0 as USB-accessible.
2. **Battery:** no battery package or holder is included; the battery is not allowed to dictate the enclosure until its actual selection and replacement approach are known.
3. **Cold shoe / heat spreader:** not included in this layout. It needs its own mounting and thermal isolation study.
4. **Wiring:** no cable, flex, connector or strain-relief envelopes are included.
5. **Fasteners:** V1.9's generic screw-head and driver envelopes are carried forward; no specific screw, insert, nut trap or thread engagement has been selected.
6. **Bushing:** axial retention remains unresolved.
7. **External controls:** no final wall cutout or finger-access volume is defined for the E8H7 wheel or lower linkage rod.
8. **Enclosure thickness:** the current Z placements are inherited from earlier reference macros. The 15 mm witness is still not a verified overall-thickness constraint.

## Macro outputs

Run `build_v20_full_internal_layout.FCMacro` in FreeCAD. Intended output names:
- `V2.0_Full_Internal_Layout.FCStd`
- `V2.0_Full_Internal_Layout.step`

The STEP export includes physical reference solids and nonphysical keep-out envelopes (screw-head, driver-access and 360-degree linkage sweep). Use the object labels to distinguish them.

## Validation status

- V2.0 macro was created from the fetched V1.9 source and committed.
- Static source checks confirmed the V2.0 document/output names, chassis footprint parameters, optical aperture cut, support posts and STEP export list.
- FreeCAD was not executed. No FCStd or STEP file was generated or inspected in this workflow.
- Boolean validity, solid intersections, actual component fit, USB-C access, tool access, optical performance, printability, structural strength and assembly sequence remain unverified.

V2.0 is a layout study only. It does not supersede the V1.7/V1.9 local rotor studies for their respective mechanisms.
