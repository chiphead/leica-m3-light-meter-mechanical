Expected outputs:

## Changes from V1.5

1. Retainer outside diameter increased from Ø28 to Ø32 mm. The four screw holes remain on PCD25.5 mm with Ø2.2 mm holes. With the boss outer radius at 15.0 mm, the hole-to-retainer-edge radial ligament becomes approximately 2.15 mm instead of 0.15 mm.
2. A shallow annular locating recess is added to the rotating flange for the upper thrust washer. The recess has 0.15 mm depth, Ø22.2 mm outer diameter and Ø20.3 mm inner diameter. It locates the washer radially; it does not yet provide positive axial capture.
3. The retainer underside height is recalculated to preserve the 0.20 mm nominal axial endplay after recessing the upper washer.

## Axial stack equation

The upper washer sits in a recess cut into the flange's upper face. Its top surface is therefore:

`Flange top Z - Pocket depth + Washer thickness`

The target gap to the stationary retainer is:

`Retainer underside Z = Flange top Z - Pocket depth + Upper washer thickness + Target endplay`

Using the current provisional values:
- Flange top Z: -6.40 mm
- Upper washer thickness: 0.30 mm
- Upper washer pocket depth: 0.15 mm
- Target endplay: 0.20 mm
- Retainer underside Z: -6.05 mm
- Retainer thickness: 1.20 mm
- Retainer OD: 32.0 mm
- Boss top / retainer seat Z: -6.05 mm
- Boss height above shell top: 2.25 mm

The lower washer still defines the rotor's lower stop. Its anti-rotation and anti-migration features are **not yet designed**. The upper washer recess only provides radial location; a positive retention method still needs review.

## Retainer support geometry

The four bosses remain Ø4.5 mm on PCD25.5 mm. Their outermost radius is 15.0 mm. With the retainer OD increased to Ø32 mm, the retainer outer radius is 16.0 mm and the screw-hole edge-to-retainer-edge radial ligament is approximately:

`16.0 - 12.75 - 1.10 = 2.15 mm`

This is a geometric improvement, not a strength certification. Boss-to-retainer bearing area, screw head seating, insert geometry, thread engagement and print orientation still need design and testing. The boss tops act as hard height stops; screw torque must not define the retainer height.

## Remaining issues

- Lower washer anti-rotation / anti-migration.
- Positive axial retention of the upper washer.
- Actual washer material, thickness tolerance, friction and wear.
- Shell/rotor/retainer tolerance stack and printed-part dimensional compensation.
- Screw head clearance, inserts, thread engagement and boss strength.
- Magnet pocket/retention, linkage rod material and swept clearance.
- Actual AS5600 sensing-face-to-magnet-surface gap.
- Final enclosure geometry and the 15 mm datum interpretation.

## Tolerance and verification

The 0.20 mm target is not guaranteed by nominal CAD values alone. A first-order worst-case stack must include measured flange-to-lower-thrust-face span, upper washer thickness, recess depth, shell underside-to-boss-seat height, and retainer seating flatness. Measure these parts rather than relying on slicer or nominal CAD dimensions.

Prototype sequence:
1. Print or machine the shell coupon, rotor, retainer and washer test pieces.
2. Measure each relevant dimension, including pocket depth and washer thickness.
3. Assemble without magnet or sensor PCB and verify free rotation through 360°.
4. Measure axial movement with a dial indicator; tune boss seat height or calibrated shims to reach the target.
5. Tighten screws against the hard stops and confirm the measured endplay does not change.
6. Test breakaway torque at multiple angular positions and inspect rubbing/wear.
7. Only then install the magnet and AS5600 and measure the real magnetic gap.

## Validation status

The FreeCAD macro has **not** been executed in this workflow. FCStd/STEP export, Boolean validity, interference checks, manufacturability and physical fit remain unverified. V1.6 is a parametric design proposal, not a manufacturing release.
