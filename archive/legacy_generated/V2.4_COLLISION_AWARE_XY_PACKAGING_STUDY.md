# V2.4 collision-aware XY packaging study

## Purpose and decision status

V2.4 compares display placement alternatives against the V2.0 component footprints and the corrected V2.3 collision findings. It is a plan-view packaging study, not a new enclosure design. All inherited dimensions are provisional envelopes unless explicitly identified as user-measured.

**No candidate in this study is approved as the functional layout baseline.** Candidate B (right-side display, USB-C to the right) is withdrawn because it violates the user's fixed left-display / left-USB requirement. Candidate A is rejected at its original coordinates because the simplified model shows internal intersections. The design must preserve the functional arrangement and grow the enclosure or add local structural volume as required.

## Reference coordinate system and fixed footprints

- X left-to-right, Y front-to-rear, Z upward.
- AS5600 / rotor axis remains at (0,0). It is a functional reference and is not moved in this study.
- V2.0 chassis inner side-wall references are approximately X=-36 and X=56 mm (2 mm rails on floor bounds -38..58).
- TSL2591 remains at the front, with its sensor facing forward (+Y).

Inherited plan-view envelopes:
- AS5600 PCB: X/Y=-11.5..11.5 mm (23 × 23 mm placeholder).
- AS5600 package: X/Y=-1..1 mm; package extends downward from PCB (Z collision already documented in V2.3).
- Rotor carrier: Ø20 mm, centered at origin.
- Rotor retaining flange: Ø24 mm, centered at origin.
- External rotor disc: Ø20 mm, centered at origin.
- Linkage rod 360-degree sweep: annulus radii 8.0..9.0 mm, centered at origin.
- TSL2591 PCB: X=-30..-14 mm, Y=23..28 mm (vertical 16 × 19 × 5 mm reference envelope; XY is only its projected plan footprint).
- E8H7 support plate: X/Y=-9..9 mm; independent encoder wheel Ø20 mm in a vertical plane, shaft axis along +Y.
- Display board: 37 × 21 mm. Its USB-C shell envelope is 3.5 mm outward from the USB-side board edge, 9 mm wide in Y, and centered on the board's local Y midpoint. These connector dimensions are provisional except for the user-measured 7.55 mm vertical relationship.

## Candidate A — display on left wall, USB-C to left

Board envelope:
- X=-36..1 mm
- Y=-14.5..6.5 mm

USB shell:
- X=-39.5..-36 mm
- Y=-8.5..0.5 mm

Disposition: **rejected**.

Reasons:
- V2.3 found 0.9 mm vertical overlap with the downward-facing AS5600 package.
- Display board intersects the rotor carrier and retaining-flange XY/Z envelopes.
- Display board intersects the V2.0 floor by 0.3 mm.
- A floor pocket would not resolve the package and rotor collisions.

## Candidate B — withdrawn: right-side display, rotated 180 degrees in-plane, USB-C toward right wall

Proposed board envelope:
- X=15.5..52.5 mm
- Y=-14.5..6.5 mm

This is the same nominal right-side display region as V2.0, shifted +2 mm in X and rotated 180 degrees in its own plane. The 180-degree rotation places the USB-C connector on the board's right edge. With the connector's provisional 3.5 mm outward projection, its shell envelope becomes:
- X=52.5..56.0 mm
- Y=-8.5..0.5 mm

This aligns the provisional shell outer face with the estimated inner face of the right chassis wall (X=56). A wall aperture would be centered at Y=-4.0 mm and at the V0.6 shell Z location relative to the LCD glass datum. The exact aperture profile must come from measuring the real shell, not from the rectangular placeholder.

### Plan-view clearance arithmetic

- To AS5600 PCB: display left edge X=15.5; AS PCB right edge X=11.5; nominal X gap = 4.0 mm.
- To rotor retaining flange: closest display point to the origin is 15.5 mm; flange radius is 12 mm; nominal radial separation = 3.5 mm.
- To rotor carrier / external disc: 15.5 - 10.0 = 5.5 mm nominal separation.
- To linkage rod sweep: 15.5 - 9.0 = 6.5 mm nominal separation.
- To TSL2591 PCB: display maximum Y=6.5; TSL PCB minimum Y=23; nominal Y separation = 16.5 mm.
- To E8H7 support plate: display left edge X=15.5; support plate right edge X=9; nominal X gap = 6.5 mm.
- To right wall inner face: display board right edge X=52.5; inner wall X=56; nominal board-to-wall gap = 3.5 mm. The USB shell projection occupies this gap by design.

These are bounding-box/radius calculations using V2.0 placeholders. They do not include display board components, solder joints, USB plug overmold, cable bend radius, structural ribs, screw bosses, cover retainers, or manufacturing tolerance.

### Functional questions before Candidate B can be accepted

1. **Display orientation:** 180-degree in-plane rotation reverses the screen orientation. Confirm the LCD controller/firmware can rotate the image 180 degrees without unacceptable UI or viewing consequences.
2. **USB access:** confirm that a right-side USB-C port is acceptable for charging/programming and that the actual plug and cable can be inserted and bent without hitting the camera body, cold shoe, or hand.
3. **Board orientation:** verify the actual component-side and underside component locations. A 37 × 21 × 8 mm envelope is not a safe support map.
4. **USB geometry:** measure shell width, height, depth, shell-to-board edge offset and actual connector opening profile. The 7.55 mm vertical datum remains the only user-measured connector relationship.
5. **Z stack:** V2.4 does not resolve the AS5600 package/magnet/rotor vertical stack. Candidate B's XY separation avoids the known plan-view overlap, but its PCB body and supports still need a coherent glass-face Z datum.
6. **External body interface:** verify right-side wall location against the camera's actual shoe/clearance and desired control access.

## Candidate C — right-side display, unrotated, USB-C faces inward

This preserves the V2.0 display orientation but places the USB shell inboard toward the central rotor. The board footprint itself clears the rotor flange by 1.5 mm if its left edge remains X=13.5, but the connector shell occupies X=10..13.5 and Y=-8.5..0.5. Its XY projection intersects the Ø24 mm retaining-flange footprint. The shell's provisional Z range also overlaps the flange Z range in the V2.0 stack.

Disposition: **not preferred; connector/rotor collision risk**. It would require relocating the board or redesigning the connector approach, not simply adding a wall aperture.

## User-confirmed layout constraint correction (2026-11-02)

The user explicitly confirms that **the display must remain on the left side and its USB-C connector must also face the left side**. Therefore Candidate B (right-side display, 180-degree in-plane rotation, USB-C to the right) violates the product requirement and is withdrawn. It must not be treated as the preferred design.

Candidate A remains rejected at its original coordinates because it intersects the AS5600 package, rotor and floor. The requirement itself is not rejected; the left-side layout must be repackaged.

### Candidate D — preserve left display and left USB, move the complete display module farther left

A first arithmetic-only alternative is to move the complete left-facing display module farther outward, rather than placing its right edge at X=1:

- Display board: X=-51..-14 mm, Y=-14.5..6.5 mm.
- USB shell: X=-54.5..-51 mm, Y=-8.5..0.5 mm.
- Proposed left-wall inner face: X=-54.5 mm.
- With a 2 mm wall, provisional floor/wall outer boundary would need to extend to about X=-56.5 mm.

Nominal plan-view separations using the current V2.0 placeholders:
- Display board to AS5600 PCB: 2.5 mm (board right edge -14; AS PCB left edge -11.5).
- Closest display-board point to rotor flange: 2.0 mm (nearest point at radius 14; flange radius 12).
- Display board to rotor carrier: 4.0 mm (14 - 10).
- Display board to linkage-rod sweep: 5.0 mm (14 - 9).
- Display board to E8H7 support plate: 5.0 mm (board right edge -14; support plate right edge +9).
- Display board to TSL2591 PCB in Y: 16.5 mm (display max Y=6.5; TSL min Y=23).

This preserves the left-side display and left-facing USB-C port while moving the display PCB's right edge clear of the central rotor's nominal Ø24 mm flange footprint. It increases the chassis width to the left by approximately 18.5 mm relative to the V2.0 floor minimum X=-38, before final wall and edge-clearance optimization.

### Candidate D is not yet accepted

The 2.0 mm flange clearance is small and is calculated from idealized envelopes. It does not include:
- actual display-board components, solder joints, glass/bezel overhang or support hardware;
- AS5600 package and PCB edge/component tolerances;
- actual rotor runout, axial play, linkage rod deflection or fastener/retainer envelope;
- USB plug overmold, insertion/removal motion and cable bend radius;
- left-side camera-body/cold-shoe interference and hand access;
- the corrected global Z stack.

The 2.5 mm display-to-AS5600-PCB plan gap is not a guarantee of 3D clearance. The AS5600 package extends below its PCB and the full vertical arrangement still needs reconciliation.

## Design-principle correction — preserve the established functional layout (2026-11-02)

The user clarifies that this product does **not** pursue minimum possible volume. Do not change the principle layout merely to make the enclosure smaller or to force a compact package.

The following are functional layout constraints, not optional optimization variables:
- Display remains on the left side.
- USB-C remains on the left side and faces the left exterior wall.
- TSL2591 remains in the forward metering position with its sensing direction unchanged.
- AS5600 remains coaxial with the magnet and rotor.
- The rotor/linkage relationship to the Leica shutter-dial mechanism remains unchanged.
- E8H7 remains an independent side-operated mechanism and is not mechanically coupled to the AS5600.
- PCB assemblies are supported by the chassis or dedicated brackets; PCBs are not structural members.

### How to resolve packaging conflicts

Do not reverse the display, move it to the right side, turn USB-C toward the opposite wall, change the sensor's viewing direction, or alter the control logic to save volume.

Instead:
1. Preserve the above functional arrangement and the established relative placement wherever possible.
2. Increase the enclosure's external dimensions and internal volume as required.
3. Add local structural volume, wall offsets, or component-specific support features where needed.
4. If two fixed functional envelopes still physically intersect, identify the exact interfering features and resolve only that local conflict. Do not silently move an entire subsystem or change its role.
5. Keep the full rotor/linkage swept envelope and service access; do not reduce clearances to chase a smaller housing.

### Status correction for Candidate D

Candidate D's proposed 15 mm leftward translation is **not an approved layout change**. It was an arithmetic exploration only and must not be treated as the new baseline. The next iteration must start from the user's established left-display/left-USB arrangement, preserve the functional placement, and grow the enclosure around it. Candidate D's nominal 2 mm rotor-flange clearance is not a reason to adopt that translation.

The V2.0/V2.2 collision findings remain valid for their modeled coordinates: they show that the current simplified 3D arrangement is internally inconsistent. Enlarging only the outer shell cannot fix an internal intersection. The next engineering task is therefore to preserve the functional layout, inspect actual component-level envelopes and Z heights, then make the smallest local structural/placement correction necessary without changing the principle layout.
