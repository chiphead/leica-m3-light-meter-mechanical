# V3.0 E8H7 orientation and exposed-wheel correction

Status: **corrected conceptual preview; not a validated enclosure or manufacturing model**.
Branch: `cla`
Generator: `generate_v2_9_functional_layout_preview.py` (retained filename for continuity; output remains V2.9 preview filename until a later clean versioned rename).

## Why V2.9 is rejected

The V2.9 generator incorrectly placed the E8H7 shaft along +X, put its wheel in the wrong plane, placed the wheel as an ordinary side-mounted cylinder without an enclosure-edge relationship, and put the TSL2591 sensor center above the encoder shaft axis. This contradicts the earlier integrated E8H7 reference and the user's explicit packaging requirements. V2.9 geometry must not be used as a layout baseline.

## Restored E8H7 mechanism

The earlier V1.3/V1.4/V1.7 reference macros consistently define:
- E8H7 shaft axis horizontal along **+Y**.
- The approximately Ø20 mm operating wheel lies in a **vertical XZ plane**.
- Wheel rotation and axial press act on the same shaft/wheel.
- E8H7 is independent of the AS5600 camera-dial rotor.
- The E8H7 push reaction must be carried by its own support into the enclosure, not through the AS5600 PCB.

V3.0 restores the +Y shaft direction and vertical XZ wheel plane. The E8H7 body is placed behind the front edge reference. The wheel is placed beyond that edge, with its wheel plane outside the housing boundary so the wheel protrudes and remains finger-accessible.

## Metering sensor vertical relationship

The TSL2591 remains vertical and forward-facing (+Y), but its sensor center is now at Z=0 while the E8H7 shaft/wheel axis is at Z=8 mm. Therefore the metering sensor center is below the dial axis in the global Z coordinate system. This is a relative layout constraint, not a claim that the complete optical module or housing is collision-free.

## Other preserved constraints

- Landscape Waveshare ESP32-S3-LCD-1.47B on the left; glass outer face Z=0 and board below it.
- USB-C on the left, facing -X.
- TSL2591 faces +Y.
- AS5600 PCB horizontal, package faces down.
- AS5600 package, magnet and one-piece bottom rotor share the same nominal axis.
- Bottom rotor remains a single conceptual rotating part.
- E8H7 remains independent from the AS5600 mechanism.
- The enclosure is allowed to grow; do not compress or rearrange functional relationships to force a smaller envelope.

## Preview reference values (still provisional)

- E8H7 body center: X=34, Y=22, Z=8 mm.
- E8H7 shaft axis: +Y, at Z=8 mm.
- E8H7 shaft projection: 2.5 mm placeholder.
- Wheel: Ø20 × 1.5 mm placeholder, vertical XZ plane.
- Front housing edge reference: Y=27 mm.
- Wheel center Y is derived from body front face + shaft projection + half wheel thickness; the wheel plane lies outside the front edge reference.
- TSL2591 board center: X=-31, front face Y=27, center Z=0 mm.
- LCD/AS5600/magnet/rotor placeholder dimensions remain unchanged from the earlier preview.

The thin front-edge marker in the preview is a **datum/reference only**, not a designed enclosure wall. Its purpose is to make the wheel's outside-the-edge relationship visible. A complete shell, finger envelope, wheel axial stroke, E8H7 support bracket, fasteners and tool access remain to be designed.

## Regeneration and checks

Run in the mechanical-design directory with CadQuery >= 2.4:

```bash
python generate_v2_9_functional_layout_preview.py
```

The script prints the E8H7 shaft direction, wheel center/front-edge relationship, and TSL2591-vs-dial Z relationship. It exports STEP and STL preview files.

No FreeCAD execution, solid validity check, full interference check, tolerance analysis, physical fit, optical validation or camera-body fit is claimed. The current change corrects the source-level orientation and relative placement only.
