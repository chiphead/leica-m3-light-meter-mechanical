## Coordinate convention

- Incident-light/front face: Y=0.
- Light travels from negative Y toward positive Y.
- Front plate occupies Y=0..2 mm.
- Common sensor active-plane reference: Y=8 mm.
- All coupons share an 18 x 18 mm plate, 2 mm thickness and 4 x 4 mm through-aperture.

## Comparison geometries

- **A — flush aperture:** 4 x 4 mm through-hole, no recess or rear sleeve.
- **B — front counterbore:** 7 x 7 mm recess, 1.5 mm deep from the incident-light face, stepping down to the common 4 x 4 mm through-hole.
- **C — rear baffle:** hollow sleeve with 7 x 7 mm outside dimensions, extending 4 mm from the plate's sensor side.

B and C are different structures: B modifies the incident-side opening; C adds a rearward sleeve toward the sensor. The macro does not include a sensor holder and cannot guarantee that a real PCB is located at the datum. Use a rigid jig to locate the actual TSL2591 optical active plane consistently for every coupon.

All dimensions are provisional fixture values, not copied from the Curious Scientist reference design and not released manufacturing dimensions.

## Test procedure

1. Inspect and validate each solid in FreeCAD before printing.
2. Mount the actual TSL2591 module in a rigid jig and keep its active plane at the same Y datum for every coupon.
3. Keep source spectrum, distance, alignment, integration time and gain fixed.
4. Record raw full-spectrum and IR channels separately at 0, 10, 20, 30 and 45 degrees incidence.
5. Repeat with a bright source just outside the intended field to assess stray-light pickup.
6. Rotate the sensor/coupon assembly around its optical axis to detect asymmetric response.
7. Compare raw channel ratio, total counts and computed EV.

Do not add a transparent window unless its transmission is characterized over both sensor response bands and the final assembly is calibrated with that exact window installed.
