# V2.8 Physical Measurement Protocol and Datum Register

Status: measurement procedure and blank data register; no new physical measurements are claimed.
Branch: `cla`
Scope: `mechanical_design/` only.

## 1. Purpose

V2.7 traced the inherited dimensions and marked the values that must be measured. V2.8 turns that list into a repeatable measurement procedure. This document is intentionally a blank measurement record: values must be entered from the actual modules, not copied from older CAD macros.

Do not create a new integrated enclosure baseline until the priority measurements below are recorded. Do not shrink or rearrange the functional architecture to make provisional dimensions fit; grow the enclosure where required.

## 2. Measurement rules

For every entry:

- Record the physical item, exact model/revision, measurement tool, units, measurement faces, measured value, uncertainty/resolution, and date.
- Take at least three independent readings for dimensions that can be measured repeatedly. Record the range; do not silently average a visibly inconsistent part.
- Photograph the part with a ruler/caliper visible and identify the exact face/edge used as the datum. Include a top view and side view for asymmetric parts.
- Distinguish PCB outline from component envelope, glass from bezel, connector shell from receptacle opening, and package body from sensing face.
- Do not infer hidden dimensions from a perspective photograph.
- Mark a value **measured** only when the physical part was measured. Datasheet values must be marked **datasheet** and linked to the exact revision.
- Keep nominal dimensions, tolerance, and measured sample dimensions in separate fields.
- If a measurement is inaccessible or ambiguous, mark it **unresolved** and describe why. Do not substitute a convenient estimate.

Suggested tools: digital caliper with verified zero, steel rule for larger envelopes, depth gauge or height gauge for stack heights, feeler gauges for assembled clearances, and a flat reference plate. Avoid using caliper jaws to compress flexible connectors or thin PCB components.

## 10. Measurement readiness and gates

### Gate 1 — before rebuilding integrated layout

- [ ] LCD PCB, glass and component envelope recorded.
- [ ] USB shell complete external profile and plug envelope recorded.
- [ ] E8H7 exact body, shaft and press-travel data recorded or explicitly unresolved.
- [ ] Measurement photographs attached or referenced.

### Gate 2 — before freezing magnetic/rotor stack

- [ ] AS5600 package sensing face and board keep-outs measured.
- [ ] Actual magnet selected and measured.
- [ ] Magnet polarity and magnetic suitability checked.
- [ ] Bushing and rotor mating dimensions selected as a matched interface.
- [ ] Axial thrust interfaces and retention method defined.

### Gate 3 — before enclosure CAD release

- [ ] TSL2591 board and sensing plane measured.
- [ ] 360-degree external linkage sweep and camera clearance checked.
- [ ] USB insertion/removal and cable bend envelope checked.
- [ ] E8H7 press load path and tool access checked.
- [ ] Bottom-shell load paths and PCB-independent supports defined.
- [ ] Battery chosen or its flexible reserved volume documented.
- [ ] Cold-shoe and heat-spreader interfaces included without unsupported thermal claims.

## 12. What happens after measurements are supplied

1. Update this register with values, uncertainty and photo references; preserve previous entries in a dated revision note if a measurement changes.
2. Recalculate the LCD/USB stack from the measured faces.
3. Rebuild the AS5600-package/magnet/rotor axis stack from the actual sensing face and magnet surface.
4. Re-evaluate the V1.7 thrust stack with actual washer, bushing and retainer dimensions.
5. Reposition/support the left-side display and independent E8H7 control without changing their functional relationships.
6. Expand the enclosure as needed. Do not compress mechanism clearances to meet an arbitrary external size.
7. Only then author a new integrated FreeCAD macro, execute it in FreeCAD, and separately validate shape validity, interference, export and physical coupons.

No FreeCAD macro was executed and no physical component was measured in this V2.8 document.
