## Purpose

This version replaces the V1.2 E8H8 external-mount SMT encoder concept with the user's actual selected model:

**F-SWITCH E8H7-6.5J30-9B15-F200**

It is a coarse spatial reference assembly, not a PCB footprint or manufacturing design. Encoder pins, solder pads, exact insertion depth, and detailed PCB outline are intentionally omitted as requested. The encoder is represented by a simplified body envelope, nominal shaft and the existing approximately Ø20 mm printed wheel.

## Coordinate convention

- XY is the horizontal plane; +Z points toward the top of the enclosure.
- AS5600 PCB is horizontal. Its sensor package is on the underside and faces -Z.
- Magnet is directly below the AS5600 package and shares its center axis.
- The bottom stepped rotor is modeled as one fused conceptual solid: magnet carrier, flange, journal, external operating disc and Ø1 mm linkage rod. This is a geometry-level one-piece concept; manufacturing method and strength remain unresolved.
- E8H7 is a separate top-menu encoder. Its shaft axis is horizontal (+Y); its thin operating wheel lies in a vertical plane.
- The E8H7 menu wheel and the AS5600 camera-dial rotor are **different mechanisms and different axes**. They must not be mechanically coupled.
- The encoder support is independent of the AS5600 PCB and must transfer the axial click load into the enclosure structure.
- The Waveshare ESP32-S3-LCD-1.47B is shown as a separate lateral board envelope, screen facing upward.
- TSL2591 is shown vertically at the front with its sensing face toward +Y. This is only a location reference; preserve an unobstructed optical path.

### Layout assumptions, not confirmed dimensions

- E8H7 reference body plan 7.8 × 6.9 mm; body block height is represented by the 6.5 mm installation-height designation for spatial planning only.
- E8H7 shaft projection 2.5 mm and shaft-axis height are placeholders.
- E8H7 reference board 12 × 12 × 1.2 mm is only a datum plane, not a verified footprint.
- Wheel thickness 1.5 mm and hub 5 mm OD × 2 mm are placeholders. The hub is fused geometrically to the wheel with a small overlap in this reference model; the actual wheel-to-Ø3 mm shaft retention method is unresolved.
- AS5600 PCB 23 × 23 × 1.6 mm, package projection 1.5 mm, magnet Ø6 × 2 mm and 1.0 mm package-face-to-magnet-face gap are placeholders.
- Rotor flange, journal, external disc, linkage rod and their positions are conceptual carry-overs, not tolerance-controlled parts.
- TSL2591 board and sensor-package dimensions are reference envelopes only.
- Display-board lateral position is arbitrary and does not lock enclosure length or width.

## 15 mm stack interpretation

The macro draws a 15 mm witness from the AS5600 PCB underside datum (Z=0) to Z=15 mm. This is **not yet a verified interpretation of the user's 15 mm measurement**. Before fixing the enclosure, confirm the exact start and end faces meant by the 15 mm allowance. The encoder board is positioned using the 6.5 mm designation and a provisional board thickness; the assembly still requires physical sample measurements.

The E8H7 wheel projects laterally beyond the encoder PCB and has a Ø20 mm swept envelope. Do not interpret the 15 mm stack target as limiting the wheel's diameter or its lateral finger-clearance envelope.

## Mechanical requirements retained

1. E8H7 wheel rotation and axial click actuation use the same wheel/shaft.
2. A mechanical stop must limit axial travel to the actual E8H7 specification; 0.2 mm is only a provisional value until the exact drawing is checked.
3. Encoder push reaction must go through an independent bracket into the enclosure, not through the AS5600 PCB, its solder joints, or the display PCB.
4. AS5600 chip and magnet must remain coaxial. Measure the gap from the actual package sensing face to the magnet surface.
5. Bottom rotor is represented as one fused solid including the vertical linkage rod. It must rotate freely with independently controlled radial guidance and axial endplay. The lower thrust interface and retainer load path remain unresolved.
6. The TSL2591 optical opening and sensor view must remain clear of the encoder wheel, rotor and structural supports.
7. The LCD glass must remain close to the upper enclosure surface; this assembly reference does not define its final Z position.
8. Battery dimensions and final enclosure length/width are not fixed by this model.
