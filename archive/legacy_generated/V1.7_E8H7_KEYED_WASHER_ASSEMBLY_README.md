# V1.7 E8H7 keyed thrust-washer assembly

Run `build_v17_e8h7_keyed_washer_assembly.FCMacro` in FreeCAD.

## Changes from V1.6
- Lower washer: an underside annular collar surrounds the Ø24 mm washer. Collar ID Ø24.2 gives 0.1 mm nominal radial clearance. An integral key engages a matching washer notch.
- Upper washer: the 0.15 mm-deep flange recess remains. An integral flange key engages a matching notch so the washer follows the rotor instead of relying on friction.
- Retainer remains Ø32 mm; nominal axial endplay target remains 0.20 mm.

The keys are geometric concepts, not validated snap features. Washers are installed axially with their notches aligned. The lower washer is axially trapped only when the rotor is assembled; it can be removed when disassembled. The upper washer is trapped between flange and stationary retainer in the assembled mechanism. Neither washer is permanently locked to its supporting part.

## Axial stack
The lower washer remains between shell underside and external rotor disc. The collar is outside the washer OD and does not intentionally change the thrust-face Z datum. The upper washer remains recessed in the rotating flange.

`E = H - S + L + P - U`

- E: axial endplay
- H: shell underside to retainer underside distance
- S: rotor external-disc upper face to flange upper face span
- L: lower washer thickness
- P: upper washer pocket depth
- U: upper washer thickness

Nominal values: H=4.25 mm, S=4.20 mm, L=0.30 mm, P=0.15 mm, U=0.30 mm; therefore E=0.20 mm.

## Initial fit assumptions
- Lower collar ID Ø24.2 around lower washer OD Ø24.0: 0.20 mm diametral clearance.
- Upper pocket OD Ø22.2 around washer OD Ø22.0: 0.20 mm diametral clearance.
- Key width 1.0 mm; notch width 1.15 mm; nominal total side clearance 0.15 mm.
- Confirm washer dimensions/material before manufacture. Key engagement and print tolerances remain provisional.
- Collar is outside the Ø20 mm rotating external-disc envelope; verify adjacent enclosure and linkage clearances.

## Required prototype checks
1. Verify collar/key and washer notch geometry, including installation alignment.
2. Confirm the upper key does not project above the washer thrust face.
3. Check usable thrust annulus and inspect key roots for print defects.
4. Measure axial movement; do not infer endplay from CAD alone.
5. Check free 360-degree rotation and breakaway torque at multiple angular positions.
6. Confirm the upper washer does not rub the stationary retainer except at the upper axial stop.

## Tolerance-stack measurement
Measure H, S, L, U and P on the actual parts, then calculate `E = H - S + L + P - U`. The initial 0.15–0.25 mm acceptance window is provisional. If outside it, adjust a controlled shim or retainer hard-stop height and remeasure after screw tightening.

## Validation status
The macro has not been executed in FreeCAD in this workflow. FCStd/STEP export, Boolean validity, key fit, interference checks, printability and physical fit remain unverified. V1.7 is a mechanical concept, not a manufacturing release.
