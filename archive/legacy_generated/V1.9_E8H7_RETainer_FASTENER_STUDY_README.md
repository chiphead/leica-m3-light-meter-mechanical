# V1.9 E8H7 retainer fastener and linkage envelope study

## Purpose

V1.9 extends the V1.7 reference assembly with:
- shallow provisional screw-head seats in the stationary retainer;
- separate screw-head solids;
- cylindrical driver-access keep-out envelopes;
- a 360-degree annular keep-out envelope for the rotating Ø1 mm lower linkage rod.

It is a study model, not a selected fastener design or a manufacturing release.

## New provisional parameters

| Feature | Value | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Retainer head seat | Ø4.2 × 0.65 mm | Shallow counterbore placeholder |
| Screw head envelope | Ø3.8 × 0.60 mm | Generic head envelope, not a specific screw |
| Driver access envelope | Ø5.5 × 8.0 mm | Cylindrical keep-out above each screw |
| Fastener pattern | 4 × Ø2.2 mm on PCD25.5 | Carried forward from V1.7 |
| Linkage rod | Ø1.0 mm at 8.5 mm radius | Carried forward from V1.7 |

The head-seat floor leaves nominal 0.55 mm of the 1.2 mm retainer thickness. The screw-head envelope is positioned flush with the retainer top in this simplified study. Actual screw head geometry, drive recess, head bearing surface and required engagement are unknown.

## Static plan-view checks

- Head-seat outer radius from the rotor axis: 12.75 + 2.10 = 14.85 mm. Against the retainer outer radius 16.0 mm, the nominal radial edge margin is 1.15 mm.
- Driver keep-out outer radius: 12.75 + 2.75 = 15.50 mm. The nominal radial margin to the retainer perimeter is 0.50 mm.
- Adjacent screw centers on the four diagonal positions are about 18.03 mm apart, greater than the Ø5.5 mm driver envelope. The four driver cylinders therefore do not overlap one another in plan view.
- These are 2D arithmetic checks only. They do not establish tool approach through a finished enclosure, tool handle clearance, screw torque access or absence of collisions with nearby components.

## Linkage rod sweep

The macro creates an annular-cylinder keep-out volume with:
- inner radius 8.0 mm;
- outer radius 9.0 mm;
- height 10.0 mm;
- Z start derived from the V1.7 linkage-rod placement.

This represents the volume swept by the center-offset vertical rod during a full 360-degree rotation, using the rod's 0.5 mm radius. It is a conservative geometric keep-out for the rod only. It does not include the external operating disc, user finger envelope, chassis walls, or deflection/runout allowance.

## Important unresolved fastening details

1. The Ø2.2 mm holes are still simple clearance bores in the current support geometry. No M2 thread, heat-set insert, nut trap or captive nut has been selected.
2. No screw length or thread engagement can be specified until the support material and fastening method are chosen.
3. The shallow head seats are not proof that a real screw head will fit. Confirm the actual screw head diameter, height, drive type and seating style.
4. The driver cylinder is only an access keep-out. It does not model a screwdriver bit, handle, angled approach or required torque.
5. Boss/retainer hard-stop seating remains a nominal contact concept. The 0.20 mm endplay must be established by measured stack dimensions or controlled shims, not by tightening torque.
6. Bushing axial retention remains unresolved.
7. The full enclosure, external disc sweep, linkage rod opening and finger access remain to be designed.

## FreeCAD macro

Run `build_v19_e8h7_retainer_fastener_study.FCMacro` in FreeCAD.

The macro exports the assembly and the new screw-head, driver-access and linkage-sweep envelopes to V1.9 FCStd/STEP filenames. The STEP export includes keep-out solids; distinguish them from physical parts by their labels. No FreeCAD execution was performed in this workflow, so the macro's runtime behavior and exports are not verified.
