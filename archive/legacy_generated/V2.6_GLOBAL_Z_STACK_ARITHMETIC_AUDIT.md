# V2.6 Global Z-stack arithmetic audit

## Purpose

This revision evaluates the existing V2.0 source equations against the V0.6 LCD glass datum. It does not select a new XY layout, change functional placement, or generate CAD. The objective is to identify which Z values are consequences of the provisional rotor stack and which interfaces remain unresolved.

## Coordinate datum

Use the LCD glass outer surface as global Z=0, positive Z upward. The following V0.6 display values are carried forward:
- Glass outer face: Z=0.
- Glass thickness: 0.6 mm (provisional).
- Display board envelope: Z=-8.6..-0.6 mm (based on nominal 8 mm overall height; verify against the actual module).
- USB-C shell far-side lower edge: Z=-7.55 mm (user-measured from glass outer face).
- USB-C shell height: 3.2 mm provisional, giving an upper edge at Z=-4.35 mm.

The measured 7.55 mm is not the receptacle internal opening datum and must not be reinterpreted as such.

- AS5600 PCB bottom Z=0, thickness 1.6 mm.
- Sensor package drop below PCB bottom=1.5 mm.
- Package-face-to-magnet-surface gap=1.0 mm.
- Magnet thickness=2.0 mm.
- Rotor carrier thickness=2.0 mm.
- Rotor flange thickness=2.0 mm.
- Journal length=2.4 mm.
- External rotor disc thickness=2.0 mm.
- Lower thrust washer thickness=0.3 mm.

## Findings

### 1. The chassis floor is derived from the provisional rotor stack

The floor bottom is computed from the external rotor disc and lower washer. It is not an independently chosen enclosure datum. If the rotor stack changes after real magnet, bushing, washer or retainer measurements, the floor Z changes with it.

Do not preserve the floor at Z=-10.3..-8.3 merely because V2.0 generated it.

### 2. LCD board and floor overlap by 0.3 mm

The display board extends down to Z=-8.6. The floor extends up to Z=-8.3. Their Z intervals overlap by 0.3 mm wherever their XY footprints intersect.

A local floor relief could theoretically remove this particular 0.3 mm overlap while retaining most of the nominal 2 mm floor thickness, but this is not yet an approved fix:
- the display XY placement has not been approved;
- safe PCB support/contact zones are unknown;
- the display also intersects the AS5600 package and rotor envelopes at historical Candidate A coordinates;
- the actual floor needs load-path and stiffness review after any relief.

A shallow floor relief alone does not solve the larger internal conflicts.

### 3. Rotor flange and display board have a separate, much larger overlap at Candidate A

At historical Candidate A (board X=-36..1, Y=-14.5..6.5), the board footprint intersects the rotor's central XY envelope. The board Z range also intersects the flange and carrier Z ranges. This cannot be resolved by changing floor thickness or adding a local floor pocket.

This rejects Candidate A's coordinates, not the required left-side display/left-facing USB functional arrangement.

### 4. AS5600 gap is measured from the package face

V2.0 places the package bottom at Z=-1.5 and magnet top at Z=-2.5, giving the 1.0 mm placeholder gap. The correct physical measurement is from the actual underside package face to the actual magnet top surface. Do not measure from the AS5600 PCB laminate.

### 5. V2.0 does not provide a validated common stack for the display and sensor

The source places the display at Z=0..8, while V0.6 defines its glass outer face at Z=0 and board below it. Therefore, the existing V2.0 LCD object and support pads are not a valid representation of the actual display stack. They must be rebuilt after component-level measurement and XY keep-outs are available.

## Required measurements before revising the stack

| Assembly | Measurement required | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| LCD | Actual glass outer face to PCB underside | Establishes real board Z envelope |
| LCD | Glass/bezel outline and protrusion | Defines cover window and retention |
| LCD | Safe underside support zones and component heights | Prevents support contact with components/solder |
| USB-C | Full shell profile relative to glass datum | Defines wall opening correctly |
| USB-C | Plug overmold and cable exit/bend envelope | Defines wall thickness and service space |
| AS5600 | PCB thickness and package-face position | Establishes sensor Z datum |
| Magnet | Actual thickness and installed top-surface Z | Establishes real magnetic gap |
| Rotor | Actual carrier/flange/journal/external disc stack | Determines floor and support positions |
| Bushing/thrust stack | Installed heights and axial endplay | Determines floor/rotor interface |
| Bottom shell | Required residual floor thickness under local reliefs | Protects structural load path |

## Decisions

- Retain the LCD glass outer face as the global Z datum.
- Do not freeze V2.0's floor Z.
- Do not freeze V2.0's LCD support-pad geometry.
- Do not use a floor pocket as a purported complete fix for Candidate A.
- Preserve the left display / left USB functional arrangement. Enlarge the enclosure and redesign local structure as required.
- Keep the rotor, AS5600 and magnet coaxial and preserve the existing mechanical function.
- Do not proceed to detailed enclosure geometry until the real component envelopes and global stack have been measured.

## Validation boundary

Source equations were inspected and evaluated arithmetically. No FreeCAD macro was executed, no CAD solids were generated, and no physical dimensions other than the stated user-measured 7.55 mm datum are newly validated. All remaining values are provisional as marked. This document is contained within `mechanical_design/`.
