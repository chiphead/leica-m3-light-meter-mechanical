# V2.1 display / USB-C interface constraint audit

## Purpose

V2.1 is a constraint audit between the V0.6 display-interface reference and the V2.0 full internal layout. It intentionally does not add a speculative USB-C cut to the chassis. The current coordinate systems do not place the display connector at an enclosure side wall, and the display Z datum in V2.0 conflicts with the previously established glass-face datum.

## Authoritative project-specific interface datum

The following values are carried forward from the V0.6 reference macro and prior physical measurement notes:

- Board envelope: 37 × 21 × 8 mm (approximate).
- LCD glass outer face: Z = 0.
- Glass thickness in V0.6 reference: 0.6 mm (provisional).
- Board body occupies Z = -8.6..-0.6 mm when the glass outer face is Z=0.
- USB-C connector is on the board's left X edge.
- USB metal-shell envelope: 9.0 mm across Y, 3.2 mm high in Z, 3.5 mm projection along X (all provisional).
- USB metal-shell far lower edge: Z = -7.55 mm, measured from the glass outer surface.
- Derived shell lower/upper edges: Z = -7.55 / -4.35 mm.
- Nominal side clearance used in V0.6: 0.3 mm each side.
- Derived wall aperture: 9.6 × 3.8 mm.
- Generic plug insertion/service keep-out from V0.6: 14 × 13 × 8 mm. This is only a rough envelope; cable bend radius and real plug overmold are not represented.

The 7.55 mm measurement refers to the far-side lower edge of the USB-C metal shell, not the connector opening edge.

## V2.0 coordinate conflict

V2.0 currently places the Waveshare board envelope at:

- X = 13.5..50.5 mm
- Y = -14.5..6.5 mm
- Z = 0..8 mm

This conflicts with the established interface datum in two independent ways.

### 1. Z direction is reversed

V2.0 currently models the board body above Z=0. V0.6 places the glass outer face at Z=0 and the board body below it, from approximately -8.6 to -0.6 mm. Consequently, V2.0's four display support pads, display height relationship to the floor, and any future USB-C vertical cut cannot be reused unchanged.

Do not simply move the board down by 8.6 mm without reviewing the full chassis/rotor/encoder Z stack. The base floor and support pads were derived from the existing V2.0 coordinate assumptions; the whole vertical stack needs a coordinated datum review.

### 2. Connector is not adjacent to the chassis left wall

In V2.0 the board's connector-side edge is X=13.5 mm. The chassis left boundary is X=-38 mm. The connector shell projects outward from the board's left edge, approximately toward decreasing X, but remains far inside the chassis footprint.

A side-wall aperture at X=-38 would therefore not align with the connector. A 48 mm-long access corridor would cross the interior layout and may intersect other components. No such corridor is approved or modeled.

The V0.6 macro placed the board at X=0..37 and a local wall coupon near X=-4..-2. That local coupon was an interface test fixture, not proof that the current V2.0 chassis wall is in the right place.

## Derived local connector envelope for future placement studies

If the V0.6 board-local convention is translated to the current V2.0 XY board placement (translation only, not an approved final placement):

- Board-local origin maps to (X=13.5, Y=-14.5).
- Connector shell X range: 10.0..13.5 mm.
- Connector shell Y center: -4.0 mm; Y range: -8.5..0.5 mm.
- Connector shell Z range: -7.55..-4.35 mm.
- Nominal wall cutout: 9.6 mm across Y × 3.8 mm across Z, centered at Y=-4.0, Z=-5.95 mm.
- Generic plug insertion keep-out extends approximately from X=-4.0 to 10.0 mm after translation, Y=-10.5..2.5 mm (13 mm width, centered on the board's Y centerline).

The translated plug envelope should be regenerated from the local definition, not copied as an enclosure wall cut. The connector's insertion axis and actual board edge must be physically confirmed before designing a wall opening.

## Required next measurements / decisions

1. Confirm the actual board orientation in the enclosure: which board edge is intended to face the external USB access side.
2. Confirm whether the board may be repositioned/rotated. Any change must be checked against the AS5600 PCB, TSL2591 board and optical path, encoder mechanism, rotor, wiring and display viewing direction.
3. Measure the connector shell width, height, projection, shell center relative to board outline, and connector insertion axis on the actual Waveshare board.
4. Measure a real USB-C plug head and cable overmold. Define straight insertion travel and cable bend keep-out separately.
5. Establish a single vertical datum for glass outer face, board underside, chassis floor, rotor stack and top cover before changing support-pad heights.
6. Once the connector is genuinely adjacent to a selected enclosure wall, add the through-cut and verify plug insertion/removal access.

## V2.1 disposition

- V0.6 glass-face and USB shell vertical datum retained as the interface reference.
- V2.0 display board Z placement flagged as inconsistent and not approved for enclosure detailing.
- V2.0 connector-to-wall relationship flagged as unresolved.
- No USB-C wall opening is claimed.
- No new FreeCAD geometry is claimed or generated by this audit.
- No FreeCAD execution, FCStd/STEP export, solid validity, interference or physical-fit validation has been performed.

**Decision:** do not proceed to a closed enclosure or finalize the LCD support geometry until the display XY orientation and global Z datum are reconciled.
