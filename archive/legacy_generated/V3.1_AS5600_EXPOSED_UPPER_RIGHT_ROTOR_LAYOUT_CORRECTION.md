# V3.1 AS5600 exposed upper-right rotor layout correction

## Status

This document records the user's clarified functional layout and supersedes the incorrect interpretation introduced in V3.0. It is a layout constraint correction, not a dimensioned enclosure release or a validated CAD model.

## Correct mechanism identification

The exposed upper-right dial is the **AS5600-linked shutter-dial rotor's external operating disc**. It is not the independent E8H7 menu encoder wheel.

The AS5600, magnet, rotor shaft and one-piece stepped rotor remain coaxial. The rotor disc is horizontal (disc plane XY) and rotates about the Z axis. Its external operating disc is located at the upper-right outside edge of the enclosure; part of its circular perimeter must extend beyond the enclosure boundary so it remains externally accessible.

The one-piece rotor concept continues to include the magnet carrier, retaining flange, journal, external operating disc and linkage rod. The linkage rod connects to the Leica shutter-dial lever. The rotor is not to be moved to the enclosure center merely because earlier local test coupons used the origin as their datum.

## Independent E8H7 mechanism

E8H7-6.5J30-9B15-F200 remains a separate user-operated control. It is not coaxial with, mechanically coupled to, or a substitute for the AS5600 rotor. Its position and wheel geometry must be treated independently. Do not infer E8H7 orientation from the AS5600 rotor's horizontal XY disc.

## Other retained functional constraints

- LCD remains on the left side, landscape, with USB-C facing the left exterior wall.
- TSL2591 remains forward-facing (+Y); the complete sensing module must be below the dial's Z-axis level as required by the user, while preserving its optical path.
- AS5600 PCB remains horizontal with its sensing package facing downward toward the magnet.
- The AS5600 package, magnet and rotor axis remain coaxial.
- PCB assemblies are independently supported and are not structural members.
- The enclosure may grow to preserve this arrangement. Do not shrink the exposed rotor, move it inward, or change its operating relationship to make the enclosure smaller.

## Historical source audit

- V0.9/V1.0 define the local one-piece stepped rotor stack and its external disc, journal, flange, carrier and linkage rod. Their origin-centered 36 x 36 mm shell coupons are mechanism test fixtures, not whole-enclosure placement.
- V1.3–V1.7 carry the rotor stack into E8H7 studies but keep the two mechanisms distinct.
- V2.0 places the rotor/AS5600 axis at (0,0) and uses a provisional 96 x 61 mm chassis centered at (10,1.5). This is not the user's approved upper-right exposed-disc placement and must not be used as the global XY layout baseline.
- V2.9 preview and the V3.0 correction chain contain incorrect layout interpretations and are not authoritative for the AS5600 rotor's global placement.

## Geometry still to recover or measure

No exact upper-right rotor center coordinate, final enclosure edge coordinate, protrusion amount, external disc diameter, or finger-clearance envelope is established by this correction note. Do not invent these values from the 36 x 36 mm local coupon or the provisional Ø20 mm disc. Recover them from an earlier user-approved layout artifact if available; otherwise record them as pending design inputs and dimension them from the actual camera/interface constraints.

The rotor's 360-degree sweep, linkage-rod sweep, body clearance, retaining hardware, service access and enclosure wall thickness must be checked against the complete external placement. The sensor module's full envelope—not just its optical package center—must be checked against the dial-axis height.
