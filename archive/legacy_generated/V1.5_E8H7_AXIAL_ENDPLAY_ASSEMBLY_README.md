# V1.5 E8H7 integrated assembly with explicit axial endplay

Run `build_v15_e8h7_axial_endplay_assembly.FCMacro` in FreeCAD.

Expected outputs:
- `V1.5_E8H7_Axial_Endplay_Assembly.FCStd`
- `V1.5_E8H7_Axial_Endplay_Assembly.step`

## Purpose

V1.5 corrects the V1.4 axial-retention stack by defining the rotor's lower stop and upper stop separately, and by introducing an explicit **0.20 mm nominal axial endplay target**. This is a design target for the next prototype, not a measured or validated clearance.

## Axial stack logic

The coordinate convention remains +Z toward the enclosure top.

- The lower thrust washer is stationary against the bottom-shell underside. At the rotor's lower stop, the external disc upper face touches the washer's lower face.
- The upper thrust washer is placed on the rotating flange's upper thrust face.
- The stationary retainer underside is positioned above the upper washer by the target endplay.
- The rotor can therefore move upward by the endplay amount from its lower stop until the upper washer contacts the retainer.
- The retainer is fixed to the shell bosses. Tightening its screws must not reduce the designed clearance; use a rigid seat/shoulder or calibrated shims rather than screw torque to set height.

The design equation is:

`Retainer underside Z = Flange top Z + Upper washer thickness + Target axial endplay`

The lower washer establishes the lower stop. The upper washer plus the retainer gap establishes the upper stop. This interpretation assumes the upper washer is retained with the rotating flange and the lower washer is retained against the stationary shell. Their actual anti-rotation/retention details remain to be designed.

## Nominal coordinate check

With the current provisional V1.3 rotor dimensions:
- External disc upper face: Z = -10.60 mm
- Shell underside / lower-washer upper face: Z = -10.30 mm
- Lower washer thickness: 0.30 mm
- Flange upper face: Z = -6.40 mm
- Upper washer thickness: 0.30 mm
- Retainer underside: Z = -5.90 mm
- Nominal upper-stop gap at the lower rotor position: 0.20 mm
- Retainer thickness: 1.20 mm
- Boss height above shell top: 2.40 mm

These are macro-derived coordinates, not physical measurements. The 0.20 mm endplay target should initially be treated as a prototype goal; a practical acceptance window of **0.15–0.25 mm** is a provisional test criterion, not a manufacturer specification.

## Radial fit and thrust interfaces

Current nominal diametral clearances:
- Journal Ø6.0 in bushing ID Ø6.2: 0.20 mm diametral (0.10 mm radial).
- Bushing OD Ø8.2 in shell bore Ø8.4: 0.20 mm diametral (0.10 mm radial).

These values are starting assumptions only. Printed holes often differ from CAD nominal dimensions; measure printed coupons and tune the parameters. If the bushing is press-fitted or bonded, its actual bore may shrink and should be measured after installation.

The thrust washers are still provisional Ø24/Ø8.4 × 0.3 mm (lower) and Ø22/Ø20.5 × 0.3 mm (upper). Material, friction, wear, creep and washer retention require physical evaluation.

## What this version does not solve

- Manufacturing tolerances of shell, rotor, bushing, washers and retainer seat.
- How the upper washer is positively retained to the rotating flange without rubbing loose.
- How the lower washer is prevented from rotating or migrating.
- Screw head/counterbore access, inserts, thread engagement and boss strength.
- Magnet pocket and magnet retention.
- Linkage rod material, attachment strength and swept clearance.
- Actual AS5600 sensing-face-to-magnet gap.
- Final enclosure geometry and 15 mm measurement datum.

## Assembly verification procedure

1. Measure the shell underside-to-retainer-seat distance after printing/machining.
2. Measure rotor thrust-face span and both washer thicknesses individually.
3. Assemble without the sensor PCB and magnet; verify free 360° rotation.
4. Check axial movement with a dial indicator or feeler gauges. Target 0.20 mm; initially accept 0.15–0.25 mm for the prototype.
5. Check breakaway torque at several angular positions. Look for local binding caused by shell/rotor runout.
6. Tighten retainer screws to the intended seating condition and repeat the measurement. Endplay must not change materially when screws are tightened.
7. Install magnet and AS5600 only after mechanical freedom and clearances are confirmed. Measure the actual package sensing-face-to-magnet-surface gap.

## Validation status

The FreeCAD macro has not been executed in this workflow. FCStd/STEP export, Boolean validity, interference checks, and physical fit remain unverified. V1.5 is an explicit parametric stack proposal, not a manufacturing release.
