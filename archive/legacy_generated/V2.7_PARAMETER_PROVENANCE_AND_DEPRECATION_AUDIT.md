# V2.7 Parameter Provenance and Deprecation Audit

Status: source-level parameter audit; not a CAD validation or manufacturing release.
Branch reviewed: `cla`.
Scope: `mechanical_design/` only.

## 1. Purpose and interpretation

This audit traces the important dimensions and mechanical assumptions from V0.6 through V2.0, using the versioned notes and macro source. A value appearing in a later macro is not automatically more authoritative than an earlier value. In particular, V2.0 is a coarse layout macro that carries forward many provisional dimensions and contains a display-placement assumption already rejected by later reviews.

Status labels used below:

- **User-measured / user-confirmed**: directly supplied or measured by the user; still record the measurement definition.
- **Selected model / product designation**: component identity or nominal designation, not necessarily a complete dimensional drawing.
- **Design constraint**: functional relationship or architecture to preserve.
- **Provisional placeholder**: editable model value without verified component measurement.
- **Superseded**: historical geometry that must not drive current design.
- **To measure / validate**: must be established on the actual part or prototype.

No FreeCAD macro execution, FCStd/STEP inspection, physical fit test, or manufacturing validation is claimed by this audit.

## 2. Current effective functional constraints

These are the constraints to carry into the next design iteration unless the user explicitly changes them:

1. Landscape Waveshare ESP32-S3-LCD-1.47B display on the **left** side; USB-C remains on the **left exterior wall** and faces outward through a real aperture.
2. TSL2591 remains forward-facing (+Y), with its optical path, baffling and spectral behavior validated separately.
3. AS5600 PCB remains horizontal, package face downward, with AS5600 package, magnet and rotor axis coaxial.
4. The bottom rotor remains a **single-piece stepped rotating component**: external operating disc, journal, internal retaining flange, upper magnet carrier, and linkage rod attached to the external disc. Bushing, thrust washers and stationary retainer remain separate parts.
5. E8H7 remains a separate horizontal-axis menu control. It is not mechanically coupled to the AS5600 rotor. Its press reaction must be carried into the enclosure, not through the AS5600 PCB.
6. Bottom shell carries mechanical loads. LCD, sensor and encoder PCBs are independently supported; the top cover is protective and must not clamp PCBs or react user control loads.
7. The enclosure is not constrained to minimum size. If clearances or component envelopes do not fit, increase the enclosure or local structure; do not compromise the above functional relationships, rotor sweep, service access or tool access.
8. Battery envelope remains open until an actual cell and connector are selected.
9. Preserve a replaceable commercial cold-shoe foot and an independently supported heat-spreading concept. Thermal benefit remains a hypothesis to measure, not a guaranteed performance claim.

## 4. Rotor evolution: what was actually superseded

The rotor history must be read as a sequence of changed concepts, not as a list of interchangeable dimensions:

- **V0.6** records old V0.5 values: external disc Ø20×2, journal Ø10×2, flange Ø24×2, carrier Ø20×5, bore Ø10.5. The V0.6 architecture explicitly labels them concept-only and calls for a bushing and retention redesign.
- **V0.9** introduces the stepped single-piece rotor plus separate sleeve bushing, washer and retainer. The trial values become disc Ø20×2, journal Ø6×2.4, flange Ø24×2, carrier Ø20×4, base bore Ø8.4, bushing OD8.2/ID6.2/L2. This is the point at which Ø10 journal and Ø10.5 bore cease to be current candidates.
- **V1.0** separates the lower and upper thrust interfaces. It changes the functional axial support arrangement, not merely a diameter.
- **V1.5** formalizes a 0.20 mm nominal endplay target. It is still a target, not a validated clearance.
- **V1.6/V1.7** add a retainer seat, upper washer recess, lower locating collar and keyed washer interfaces. These refine retention/anti-rotation; they do not prove manufacturability or fit.
- **V1.9** adds screw-head and driver keep-out envelopes and a 360° rod-sweep reference. These are space reservations, not verified tool access or enclosure clearance.
- **V2.0** carries the V1.7/V1.9 rotor study into a larger coordinate layout. Its repeated values are inherited assumptions, not independent confirmation.

The carrier thickness is especially easy to misread: V0.9's Ø20×4 carrier does not mean V2.0's Ø20×2 carrier has been measured or validated. V2.0's source explicitly models the upper carrier disc as 2 mm thick.

## 5. E8H7 lineage and limits

- V1.1's vertical encoder / separate center-button concept is historical and not the current selected mechanism.
- V1.2's E8H8-7.5J30-9B15-F200 external-mount SMT geometry is explicitly superseded. Its E8H8 drawing values must not be silently transferred to E8H7.
- V1.3 selects E8H7-6.5J30-9B15-F200 and uses a horizontal shaft with the user's separate thin wheel. E8H7's exact footprint and detailed body drawing remain absent from the audited macro.
- The 0.2 mm press travel in V1.3/V1.7/V2.0 is a carry-over placeholder; V1.3 itself says exact E8H7 drawing confirmation is pending. Do not design a hard stop around it as a confirmed specification.
- E8H7's click force must be reacted through its independent support and into the enclosure. It must not load the AS5600 board.

## 6. Parameters that should be measured before the next integrated CAD baseline

Priority measurements:

1. **LCD assembly:** actual PCB outline, maximum component heights, glass/bezel thickness, board-to-glass relation, safe support zones, USB metal-shell complete profile and plug envelope.
2. **USB interface:** confirm the 7.55 mm datum on the actual board; measure shell width/height/depth and position in Y/Z; check real plug insertion and cable bend radius.
3. **AS5600 assembly:** PCB outline/thickness, package underside position, package sensing-face plane, component keep-outs, actual magnet diameter/thickness/grade/polarity and magnet retention.
4. **Rotor/bushing pair:** select bushing material and actual part; measure OD/ID/length; then set journal diameter, bore and retention. Measure rotor thrust-face span and washer thicknesses.
5. **Axial stack:** measure H, S, L, P and U for the V1.7 relation E=H−S+L+P−U. Confirm endplay after fastening, not just in a loose CAD stack.
6. **E8H7:** exact product drawing or physical sample; body datum, mounting height, shaft diameter/projection, axial actuation travel, force, torque, terminal keep-outs. Confirm whether 0.2 mm applies to this exact model.
7. **TSL2591:** actual board and sensor package envelope, sensing plane, viewing angle, spectral behavior of any window, and empirically selected aperture/baffle.
8. **External interfaces:** full 360° camera linkage rod and external disc sweep, Leica body clearance, finger access, screwdriver access, and cold-shoe fastening envelope.
9. **Battery:** choose the actual cell and connector only after the above functional geometry is stable.

## 7. V2.8 recommended sequence

1. Create a measurement record for the actual LCD/USB-C assembly and actual E8H7 sample; mark every dimension with its measurement faces and uncertainty.
2. Replace the V2.0 right-side LCD placement with the required left-side landscape arrangement while retaining USB-C left-facing access. Do not try to rescue V2.0 coordinates by merely translating the board if that recreates collisions.
3. Rebuild the global Z stack from LCD glass Z=0 and measured component faces. Do not inherit V2.0 floor Z or 15 mm witness as fixed geometry.
4. Rebuild the AS5600/magnet/rotor axis stack around measured package and selected magnet. Preserve coaxiality and chip-down orientation.
5. Carry forward V1.7 keyed-washer arrangement as a concept baseline, but close bushing axial retention, washer materials, retainer fastening and measurable endplay before treating it as a buildable mechanism.
6. Place E8H7 as a separate horizontal control with a verified body/shaft envelope and an independent load path.
7. Only then grow the enclosure footprint and add USB aperture, TSL optical channel, cold shoe/heat spreader, battery carrier, cable routing and cover.
8. Run FreeCAD macro execution, shape validity, interference checks and physical coupon tests as separate explicit validation stages. Source-level arithmetic is not a substitute.
