# V2.5 Functional layout integrity review

## Decision

The product is not a minimum-volume design. Preserve the established functional arrangement and enlarge the enclosure when required. Do not change subsystem side, facing direction, axis relationship, optical direction, or user access simply to reduce dimensions.

Fixed requirements:
- Waveshare ESP32-S3-LCD-1.47B display stays on the left, landscape.
- USB-C stays on the left edge and faces the left exterior wall.
- TSL2591 stays forward-facing (+Y) in the front metering position.
- AS5600 stays horizontal, package facing down toward the magnet.
- AS5600, magnet and rotor remain coaxial.
- One-piece rotor and Leica linkage relationship remain unchanged in principle.
- E8H7 remains an independent side-operated control; it is not coupled to AS5600.
- PCB assemblies are independently supported; no PCB carries mechanical loads.
- Bottom shell carries loads. Top cover is protective and must not clamp the LCD PCB or carry control loads.
- Battery choice remains open and must not drive enclosure dimensions.

## Meaning of preserving the layout

Preserve functional side, facing direction, axis relationships, sensing direction, user access and independent load paths. This does not freeze every provisional CAD coordinate. A local coordinate adjustment is allowed only to resolve a specific identified interference without changing the subsystem's functional role or user-facing arrangement.

When space is insufficient:
1. Increase overall enclosure dimensions or local wall/shoulder volume.
2. Add dedicated brackets, standoffs, ribs or pockets.
3. Reconcile component keep-outs and the global Z stack.
4. Only then consider a minimal local adjustment that preserves the functional arrangement.

Do not move the display to the right, rotate it 180 degrees, turn USB-C to the right, move the TSL2591 away from its forward position, move the AS5600/rotor axis, or couple E8H7 to the rotor as packaging shortcuts.

## V2.0 source audit

Inspected `build_v20_full_internal_layout.FCMacro`; findings below are source-level only.

### Display location and Z datum

V2.0 sets display size 37 x 21 x 8 mm and center X=32, Y=-4. Its generated board envelope is X=13.5..50.5, Y=-14.5..6.5, Z=0..8. This is a right-side placement and conflicts with the required left-side arrangement. Do not reuse these coordinates as the product baseline.

V0.6 defines LCD glass outer surface Z=0 and board body approximately Z=-8.6..-0.6. V2.0's Z=0..8 placement is inconsistent with that datum. The 7.55 mm user measurement is from the glass outer surface to the far-side lower edge of the USB-C metal shell, not the internal receptacle opening.

### Display supports

V2.0's four support pads were generated for its right-side, Z=0..8 board placement. They have no verified contact map for the real PCB underside and are not reusable as-is. New supports must contact safe measured PCB zones, avoid components/solder/connector, and support the PCB independently of the cover.

### Floor and rotor stack

V2.2/V2.3 identify a 0.3 mm overlap between the corrected V0.6 display-board Z envelope and the inherited V2.0 floor at the historical Candidate A coordinates. The floor was derived from the provisional rotor stack; it is not an immutable datum. Reconcile floor, rotor, magnet, AS5600 and LCD glass together from glass Z=0.

The historical Candidate A at X=-36..1, Y=-14.5..6.5 intersects the downward-facing AS5600 package, rotor carrier/flange and floor. Those coordinates are rejected; this does not reject the left-side display requirement.

Candidate D's additional 15 mm left translation was only an arithmetic exploration, not an approved layout or required shift.

### Other subsystem constraints

Keep TSL2591 forward-facing with an independent optical bracket/tunnel. Its aperture, window spectral transmission and field of view remain experimental.

Keep E8H7 independent, side-accessible and supported through its own reaction path. Its exact mounting datum and body envelope remain provisional.

Preserve the one-piece rotor, separate radial bushing, thrust washers and stationary retainer. Maintain free rotation, axial control, full linkage-rod sweep and screw/tool access. Do not shrink these envelopes to accommodate the display.

## Next engineering work

1. Measure the actual LCD board outline, glass/bezel, underside components, solder zones, USB shell, plug overmold and cable bend envelope. Keep the known 7.55 mm datum distinct from unmeasured dimensions.
2. Measure AS5600 PCB and package heights, package-face datum, magnet and actual package-to-magnet gap.
3. Reconcile the rotor, magnet, AS5600, floor and LCD glass in one global Z coordinate system.
4. Preserve left display/left USB while sizing the left wall, connector opening and cable egress. Allow the enclosure to grow.
5. Detail the TSL2591 optical bracket and E8H7 independent support separately.
6. Create a static keep-out matrix, then a CAD assembly with static intersections, moving envelopes and service access checked.
7. Only after the assembly is coherent should a detailed enclosure macro be authored and executed.

## Validation boundary

This is a source/document review. No FreeCAD macro was executed; no FCStd/STEP was generated or validated; no FreeCAD shape validity, interference test, physical fit, plug/cable access, optical response or camera-body fit is claimed. The 7.55 mm LCD-to-USB-shell datum is user-measured; most inherited dimensions remain provisional. All work in this revision is confined to `mechanical_design/`.
