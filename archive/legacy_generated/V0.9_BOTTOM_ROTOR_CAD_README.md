## Assembly concept

The model separates the stationary support parts from the single-piece rotating part:
- Bottom-shell coupon with a real through-bore.
- Replaceable sleeve bushing.
- One-piece stepped rotor: external operating disc, shaft journal, internal retaining flange and upper magnet carrier.
- Separate thrust washer.
- Separate screw-fixed annular retainer.
- Ø1 mm vertical linkage rod fused to the edge of the external operating disc.

The annular retainer overlaps the outer rim of the rotor flange while its center opening clears the magnet carrier. In a real assembly it must be fixed to reinforced enclosure bosses and must not clamp the rotating flange. The model is a layout reference; fastener bosses, screw engagement, material choice and service access are not yet designed.

## Provisional geometry

All values are editable constants at the top of the macro. They are not measured final dimensions. In particular, the 6 mm journal, 8.4 mm shell bore, bushing dimensions, 0.3 mm thrust washer and retainer geometry are trial values to illustrate the stack-up. The old V0.5 journal and bore values are not treated as validated.

## Critical fit checks before printing

1. Radial running clearance is set by bushing ID minus journal diameter. Confirm the selected print/material/process can hold this clearance; do not infer a friction fit from the nominal CAD values.
2. The bushing OD must suit the actual bottom-shell bore and retention method. The macro currently shows the parts concentrically, not a validated press fit.
3. Check axial capture: the external disc and internal flange capture the shell wall; the retainer is an additional stationary keeper. Set measurable axial endplay so the rotor turns freely and the retainer cannot preload the flange.
4. Confirm the annular retainer clears the carrier throughout rotation and that its screw holes land on real reinforced bosses.
5. The rod is vertical and physically connected to the external disc. Its material must be checked for ferromagnetism and its attachment strength validated.
6. Check that the rod's swept envelope clears the camera body and the final enclosure at every rotor angle.
7. The magnet is intentionally omitted from this mechanism model. Select the real magnet and establish its pocket, polarity and package-face air gap together with the AS5600 stack. Do not use the carrier top as the sensor-gap datum.

## Important limitations

- The base is a 36 x 36 mm test coupon, not the final enclosure.
- The rotor model uses fused primitive solids; no manufacturing fillets, draft, print orientation or tolerance compensation is included.
- The macro has not been executed in FreeCAD. FCStd/STEP export and solid validity are unverified.
- No real bearing/bushing supplier part has been selected.
- Do not print this as a production part until actual component measurements and free-rotation/endplay tests are complete.
